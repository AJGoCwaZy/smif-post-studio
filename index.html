Quick rules

Do

  • Keep the logo in its original proportions.
  • Leave clear space around the logo, about the height of the letter S.
  • Use only the colors above.
  • Use real club photos, and only with the person's consent.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects to the logo.
  • Put text over busy parts of a photo.
  • Post member phone numbers, emails or other contact details.
  • Squeeze in extra text. Shorter reads better.

Save your image

Press and hold the image, then choose Save to Photos. On a computer, right click and choose Save image as.
